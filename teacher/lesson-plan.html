Preprepared Lesson Plan: Distribution to Solve for x

Part 1 (10 min): Review

Recall one-step equation solving and inverse operations.

Part 2 (15 min): Core Concept

Expand brackets correctly, then isolate x using inverse operations.

Part 3 (10 min): Guided Practice

Students solve 3(x + 2) = 18, 2(x - 4) = 10, and 4(x + 1) = 28.

Part 4 (5 min): Exit Check

One quick question to confirm readiness for word-problem lesson.
