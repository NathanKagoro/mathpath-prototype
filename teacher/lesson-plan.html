Lesson plan

Distribution to solve for x. 40 minutes.

Week 2: multi-step distribution

Objective. Students solve equations with brackets and isolate x accurately.

  1. Part 1, 10 minReviewRecall one-step equations and inverse operations.
  2. Part 2, 15 minCore ideaMultiply out the bracket, every term, then solve the two-step equation.
  3. Part 3, 10 minGuided practiceStudents solve 3(x + 2) = 18, 2(x − 4) = 10, 4(x + 1) = 28.
  4. Part 4, 5 minExit checkOne quick question to confirm the class is ready for word problems.
