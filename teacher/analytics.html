Commonest slip

Forgot to multiply out the whole bracket. 7 of the 30 homework answers, 23%.

On 2(x + 3) = 21 it looks like this:

  1. 2(x + 3) = 21
  2. 2x + 3 = 21 should be 2x + 6 = 21
  3. 2x = 18
  4. x = 9

The right answer is x = 15/2 = 7.5. The slip gives x = 9.
